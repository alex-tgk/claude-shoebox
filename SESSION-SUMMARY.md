## 🎓 Key Insights from Research

### What Works
1. **AI as accelerator**: 50-80% time savings in development
2. **Content over templates**: Users value substance over style
3. **Generous free tiers**: Builds trust, converts 3-5%
4. **Transparent pricing**: No dark patterns wins customer loyalty
5. **Outcome focus**: Prove ROI (interviews, conversions, etc.)

### What to Avoid
1. **Platform dependence**: Don't rely solely on one marketplace/API
2. **Unrealistic timelines**: "Get rich quick" doesn't work
3. **Feature bloat**: Focus on core value, ship fast
4. **Ignoring unit economics**: Must achieve LTV:CAC > 3:1
5. **Poor onboarding**: First 10 minutes determine retention

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Modern tech stack**: Next.js 15, Tailwind v4, latest best practices
2. **AI integration**: Strategic use for competitive edge
3. **Solo-friendly**: One person can build and operate
