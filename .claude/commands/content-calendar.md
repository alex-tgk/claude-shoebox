# Content Calendar Creator

Create a comprehensive content calendar for blog posts, social media, and content marketing campaigns.

## Instructions

Generate a detailed content calendar that includes:

### 1. Calendar Overview
- Time period covered (30, 60, or 90 days)
- Content pillars and themes
- Publishing frequency by channel
- Team responsibilities
- Key events and seasonal opportunities

### 2. Content Strategy Foundation
- Business objectives tied to content
- Target audience personas
- Content goals (awareness, consideration, conversion)
- Key topics and themes
- SEO keyword priorities
- Content funnel mapping (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU)

### 3. Content Types and Mix
Distribution across:
- Blog posts/articles
- Social media posts (by platform)
- Email newsletters
- Video content
- Infographics
- Podcasts
- Webinars
- Case studies
- Whitepapers
- User-generated content

### 4. Content Calendar Grid

For each piece of content, include:
- Publication date and time
- Content title/topic
- Content type and format
- Content pillar/theme
- Target audience/persona
- Buyer journey stage
- Primary keyword/SEO focus
- Distribution channels
- Author/creator assigned
- Status (idea, outline, draft, review, scheduled, published)
- Call-to-action (CTA)
- Related content/internal links
- Promotion plan

### 5. Platform-Specific Content Plans

#### Blog/Website:
- Article topics and headlines
- Word count targets
- SEO keywords
- Publishing schedule
- Content categories
- Internal linking strategy

#### Social Media by Platform:
- LinkedIn posts (professional insights, company updates)
- Twitter/X posts (real-time engagement, thought leadership)
- Instagram posts (visual storytelling, behind-the-scenes)
- Facebook posts (community building, engagement)
- TikTok/Reels (short-form video)
- YouTube (long-form video, tutorials)

For each platform:
- Posting frequency
- Best posting times
- Content themes
- Hashtag strategy
- Engagement tactics

#### Email Marketing:
- Newsletter topics
- Send schedule
- List segments
- Subject line ideas
- Content previews

### 6. Content Themes by Week/Month
- Week 1 theme
- Week 2 theme
- Week 3 theme
- Week 4 theme
- Monthly spotlight topic

### 7. Seasonal and Event-Based Content
- Industry events
- Holidays and observances
- Product launches
- Company milestones
- Trending topics and newsjacking opportunities

### 8. Content Production Workflow
- Ideation process
- Research phase
- Creation timeline
- Review and approval process
- Publishing process
- Promotion workflow

### 9. Content Repurposing Strategy
- Blog → Social posts
- Webinar → Blog + Video clips
- Research → Infographic + Social
- Case study → Multiple formats
- Podcast → Blog transcription

### 10. Resource Planning
- Writers/creators assigned
- Designers needed
- Video production requirements
- Subject matter experts
- Approval chain
- External contributors

### 11. Content Promotion Plan
- Organic social promotion
- Paid promotion budget
- Email distribution
- Influencer outreach
- Employee advocacy
- Community engagement

### 12. Performance Tracking
- Key metrics by content type
- Traffic goals
- Engagement targets
- Conversion goals
- SEO ranking goals
- Lead generation targets

### 13. Content Ideas Bank
- Future topics to develop
- Evergreen content ideas
- Seasonal content ideas
- Trending opportunities
- User questions to answer

## Output Format

Provide:
1. Strategic overview and rationale
2. Monthly content calendar in table format
3. Weekly breakdown with specific posts
4. Platform-specific posting schedules
5. Content briefs for priority pieces
6. Team assignments and deadlines
7. Metrics dashboard description

Present the calendar in an easy-to-follow table format organized by:
- Date
- Content piece
- Platform/channel
- Status
- Owner
- Notes

Include a mix of content types and ensure consistent publishing cadence.

Ask about the business, target audience, channels in use, team size, and content goals before creating the calendar.
