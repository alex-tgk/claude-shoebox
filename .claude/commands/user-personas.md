# User Persona Creator

Create detailed, research-based user personas for the target market specified by the user.

## Instructions

Develop 3-5 comprehensive user personas that include:

### For Each Persona:

#### 1. Persona Overview
- Name and photo description
- Role/job title
- Age range
- Location
- One-sentence summary

#### 2. Demographics
- Age and generation
- Gender (if relevant)
- Education level
- Income range
- Family situation
- Geographic location

#### 3. Professional Background
- Industry and company size
- Job title and seniority
- Years of experience
- Career goals and aspirations
- Team size and structure
- Reporting relationships

#### 4. Psychographics
- Personality traits
- Values and beliefs
- Attitudes and opinions
- Lifestyle preferences
- Risk tolerance
- Decision-making style

#### 5. Goals and Motivations
- Primary goals (professional and personal)
- Success metrics
- Aspirations
- What drives their decisions
- Career advancement goals

#### 6. Challenges and Pain Points
- Daily frustrations
- Workflow inefficiencies
- Resource constraints
- Knowledge gaps
- Time pressures
- Budget limitations

#### 7. Behaviors and Habits
- Daily routine
- Work patterns
- Communication preferences
- Technology usage
- Information consumption habits
- Social media activity

#### 8. Technology Profile
- Devices used
- Software and tools in their stack
- Technical proficiency level
- Preferred platforms
- Early adopter vs. late majority

#### 9. Buying Behavior
- Decision-making process
- Influencers and stakeholders involved
- Buying criteria and priorities
- Budget authority
- Evaluation process
- Objections and concerns

#### 10. Relationship to Product
- Current solutions they use
- Workarounds they've created
- Ideal solution description
- Must-have vs. nice-to-have features
- Expected outcomes and ROI

#### 11. Quote
- A memorable quote that captures their perspective
- In their own words describing their challenges

#### 12. User Journey
- Awareness stage behavior
- Consideration stage behavior
- Decision stage behavior
- Onboarding needs
- Retention factors

### Persona Comparison Matrix

Create a table comparing all personas across:
- Primary goal
- Main pain point
- Tech savviness
- Price sensitivity
- Decision authority
- Preferred communication channels

### Persona Prioritization

Rank personas by:
- Market size
- Revenue potential
- Strategic importance
- Ease of reaching
- Product-market fit strength

## Output Format

For each persona, provide:
1. Visual description (for persona card)
2. Detailed profile narrative
3. Specific, realistic details (not generic)
4. Evidence-based insights
5. Actionable implications for product/marketing

End with strategic recommendations on:
- Which personas to prioritize
- How to tailor messaging by persona
- Product features by persona priority
- Marketing channel recommendations

Ask about the product/service, industry, and any existing customer research before creating personas.
