## Output Format

Provide:
1. 30-50 specific, actionable growth hack ideas
2. Prioritized by potential impact and ease of implementation
3. Each growth hack includes:
   - Tactic name
   - Description (2-3 sentences)
   - Implementation steps
   - Required resources
   - Expected impact
   - Success metrics
   - Time to implement
   - Risk level
4. Growth experimentation roadmap (next 90 days)
5. Quick wins to start immediately (top 5)
6. Measurement and tracking recommendations

Organize ideas by:
- Funnel stage (acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral)
- Channel (content, paid, viral, etc.)
- Impact potential (high, medium, low)
- Effort required (low, medium, high)

Ask clarifying questions about the product, current growth challenges, resources, and goals before generating ideas.
