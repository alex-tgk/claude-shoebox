# Investor/Client Pitch Deck Creator

Create a compelling investor pitch deck or client presentation outline for the business or product specified by the user.

## Instructions

Generate a comprehensive pitch deck structure and content for:

### 1. Pitch Deck Overview
- Deck purpose (fundraising, sales, partnership)
- Audience (VCs, angels, corporate clients, partners)
- Stage (pre-seed, seed, Series A, etc.)
- Funding goal (if applicable)
- Presentation context (email, in-person, demo day)

### 2. Standard Pitch Deck Structure (10-15 slides)

The following structure follows best practices for investor pitch decks:

---

#### Slide 1: Cover Slide
**Content:**
- Company name and logo
- Tagline (one compelling sentence)
- Founder name(s) and title(s)
- Contact information
- Date
- Confidential notice (if applicable)

**Design Notes:**
- Clean, professional design
- Strong visual or product image
- Branded colors and fonts
- Set the tone for the deck

**Examples:**
- "Uber: Everyone's Private Driver"
- "Airbnb: Book rooms with locals, rather than hotels"

---

#### Slide 2: Problem
**Content:**
- The problem you're solving (3-4 specific pain points)
- Why this problem matters (market size, frequency, intensity)
- Current situation and why it's inadequate
- Cost of the problem (time, money, frustration)
- Who experiences this problem (your target audience)

**Best Practices:**
- Make it relatable and emotional
- Use storytelling or specific scenarios
- Include statistics that demonstrate scale
- Show you deeply understand the customer

**Examples:**
- "73% of small businesses struggle with X"
- "Users waste 10 hours per week on Y"
- Customer quotes highlighting frustration

---

#### Slide 3: Solution
**Content:**
- Your product/service in one sentence
- How it solves the problem
- Key features or approach
- Why your solution is different/better
- The "aha moment" for users

**Best Practices:**
- Keep it simple and clear
- Show the product (screenshot, mockup, or demo)
- Focus on benefits, not just features
- Demonstrate the value prop clearly

**Visual Elements:**
- Product screenshot
- Before/after comparison
- Simple diagram of how it works

---

#### Slide 4: Why Now?
**Content:**
- Market timing and trends
- Technology enablers (AI, mobile, etc.)
- Regulatory changes
- Behavioral shifts
- Recent events creating opportunity
- Why this couldn't be done 5 years ago

**Best Practices:**
- Show macro trends supporting your business
- Demonstrate urgency
- Explain your insight about the market
- Reference recent success of similar companies

---

#### Slide 5: Market Size
**Content:**
- Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- Market growth rate and projections
- Top-down and bottom-up calculations

**Best Practices:**
- Show your market is large enough
- Be realistic and credible (avoid "$1 trillion market")
- Demonstrate you understand market segmentation
- Show path to capturing market share

**Visual Elements:**
- Expanding circles diagram (TAM > SAM > SOM)
- Market growth chart
- Market size comparisons

---

#### Slide 6: Product/Demo
**Content:**
- Product walkthrough (key screens)
- Core features and functionality
- User experience highlights
- Unique innovations
- Product roadmap (current and future)

**Best Practices:**
- Show, don't tell
- Keep it high-level (details in appendix)
- Highlight what makes it special
- Use annotated screenshots
- Consider live demo (risky but impactful)

**Visual Elements:**
- Product screenshots with callouts
- User flow diagram
- Before/after comparisons
- Video demo (if email deck)

---

#### Slide 7: Business Model
**Content:**
- How you make money
- Revenue streams
- Pricing model and tiers
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV, margins)
- Path to profitability
- Monetization strategy

**Best Practices:**
- Be crystal clear on how you make money
- Show you've thought through unit economics
- Demonstrate scalability
- Address investor concerns about monetization

**Examples:**
- Subscription model with tiered pricing
- Transaction fees or marketplace commissions
- Freemium with conversion funnel
- B2B SaaS with per-seat pricing

---

#### Slide 8: Traction/Milestones
**Content:**
- Key metrics and growth
- Customer/user numbers
- Revenue (if any)
- Growth rate (MoM, YoY)
- Key milestones achieved
- Customer testimonials or logos
- Partnerships or recognition

**Best Practices:**
- Show momentum and validation
- Hockey stick growth charts
- Highlight impressive metrics
- Show proof of concept
- Include social proof

**Visual Elements:**
- Growth charts (up and to the right)
- Customer logos
- Key numbers highlighted
- Timeline of milestones

---

#### Slide 9: Competitive Landscape
**Content:**
- Competition matrix or positioning map
- Direct and indirect competitors
- Your competitive advantages
- Defensibility and moat
- Why you'll win
- Barriers to entry for competitors

**Best Practices:**
- Acknowledge competition (never say "no competition")
- Show you understand the landscape
- Demonstrate clear differentiation
- Explain your unfair advantage
- Use 2x2 matrix for positioning

**Visual Elements:**
- Competitive matrix (features comparison)
- 2x2 positioning map
- "Secret sauce" breakdown

---

#### Slide 10: Go-to-Market Strategy
**Content:**
- Customer acquisition channels
- Marketing and sales strategy
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) by channel
- Sales process and funnel
- Partnership strategy
- Geographic expansion plan

**Best Practices:**
- Show specific, actionable tactics
- Demonstrate you've tested channels
- Include realistic CAC and conversion rates
- Show path to scale

**Examples:**
- Content marketing + SEO
- Inside sales team
- Channel partnerships
- Product-led growth
- Community-driven acquisition

---

#### Slide 11: Team
**Content:**
- Founders and key team members
- Photos, names, titles
- Relevant experience and expertise
- Previous companies or exits
- Unique insights or domain expertise
- Advisors and board members
- Key hires planned

**Best Practices:**
- Show you're the right team to execute
- Highlight relevant experience
- Show complementary skill sets
- Include impressive credentials
- Demonstrate you've worked together before (if true)

**Visual Elements:**
- Professional headshots
- LinkedIn-style profiles
- Previous company logos
- Educational credentials

---

#### Slide 12: Financials
**Content:**
- Historical financial performance (if any)
- 3-5 year financial projections
- Key assumptions
- Path to profitability
- Burn rate and runway
- Unit economics

**Best Practices:**
- Show you understand the numbers
- Be realistic but optimistic
- Explain key assumptions
- Focus on metrics that matter (revenue, margins, CAC/LTV)
- Show how investment accelerates growth

**Visual Elements:**
- Revenue projection chart
- Key metrics table
- Breakdown by revenue stream
- Scenario analysis (base, optimistic, conservative)

---

#### Slide 13: The Ask
**Content:**
- Amount raising ($X million)
- Round type (Seed, Series A, etc.)
- Valuation or terms (if appropriate)
- Use of funds breakdown
- Key milestones funds will achieve
- Timeline to next round
- What success looks like

**Best Practices:**
- Be specific about the amount
- Show how money will be used
- Tie to milestones and outcomes
- Demonstrate capital efficiency
- Show this gets you to profitability or next fundraise

**Use of Funds Breakdown:**
- Product development: 40%
- Marketing and sales: 30%
- Operations and hiring: 20%
- Working capital: 10%

---

#### Slide 14: Vision
**Content:**
- Long-term vision (3-5+ years)
- Market leadership position
- Expansion opportunities
- Platform potential
- Exit potential or public market path
- Impact and mission

**Best Practices:**
- Paint compelling future picture
- Show massive opportunity
- Inspire and excite
- Show path to 10x+ returns
- Balance ambition with realism

---

#### Slide 15: Closing/Thank You
**Content:**
- Key takeaways (3 bullets)
- Contact information
- Call to action
- Website and social links
- Thank you message

---

### 3. Appendix Slides (Optional, for Reference)

Additional slides to have ready:
- Detailed product roadmap
- Detailed financial model
- Technical architecture
- Customer case studies
- Press and media coverage
- Market research data
- Team bios (detailed)
- Customer testimonials
- Intellectual property
- Regulatory considerations
- Risk factors and mitigation

### 4. Pitch Deck Variants

#### Email/Send-Ahead Deck:
- More detailed and self-explanatory
- Can be understood without presenter
- More slides okay (15-20)
- Detailed captions and notes
- Video or GIF demos embedded

#### In-Person Presentation Deck:
- Minimal text, more visuals
- Support your narrative
- 10-15 slides
- High-impact images
- Simple, bold statements

#### Demo Day Deck (3-5 minutes):
- Ultra-concise (8-10 slides)
- Only most critical information
- Large, readable fonts
- Powerful visuals
- Memorable hook

#### Client Sales Deck:
- Focus on their problems and your solution
- Case studies and ROI
- Implementation and support
- Pricing and packages
- Testimonials and social proof

### 5. Design Guidelines

#### Visual Hierarchy:
- One main idea per slide
- Large, bold headlines
- Minimal bullet points (3-5 max)
- Ample white space
- Consistent layout

#### Typography:
- Sans-serif fonts (modern, clean)
- Large font sizes (24pt+ for body, 36pt+ for headers)
- Limited font variety (2 fonts max)
- High contrast for readability

#### Color Scheme:
- Brand colors consistently applied
- High contrast text on backgrounds
- Limited color palette (3-4 colors)
- Strategic use of color for emphasis

#### Images and Graphics:
- High-quality, professional images
- Custom illustrations over stock photos
- Data visualizations over tables
- Icons for clarity
- Consistent style throughout

#### Data Visualization:
- Simple, clear charts
- Remove clutter and gridlines
- Annotate key insights
- Use color to highlight important data
- One chart per slide

### 6. Storytelling and Narrative

#### Narrative Arc:
- Hook: Compelling opening
- Problem: Pain point that matters
- Solution: Your unique answer
- Proof: Validation and traction
- Opportunity: Massive market
- Plan: How you'll win
- Team: Why you'll execute
- Ask: What you need

#### Storytelling Techniques:
- Start with customer story
- Use analogies and metaphors
- Include surprising statistics
- Create emotional connection
- Build tension and resolution
- End with inspiring vision

### 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

#### Content Mistakes:
- Too much text on slides
- Vague or generic statements
- Overclaiming or hype
- Ignoring competition
- Unrealistic financial projections
- Unclear ask or use of funds
- No clear differentiation

#### Design Mistakes:
- Cluttered slides
- Inconsistent formatting
- Low-quality images
- Hard-to-read fonts or colors
- Distracting animations
- Too many slides
- Dense tables or data

#### Presentation Mistakes:
- Reading slides verbatim
- Going over time
- Skipping the problem
- Getting lost in details
- Defensive about competition
- Unclear on business model
- Not knowing your numbers

### 8. Pitch Preparation

#### Rehearsal:
- Practice out loud 10+ times
- Time yourself (stay under limit)
- Anticipate questions
- Prepare smooth transitions
- Get feedback from others
- Record yourself

#### Q&A Preparation:
Prepare answers for:
- Why you? Why now?
- How is this defensible?
- What if [big company] does this?
- What's your customer acquisition strategy?
- How do you make money?
- What are the risks?
- Why will you succeed where others failed?
- What's your go-to-market strategy?
- How much traction do you have?
- What will you do with the money?

### 9. Industry-Specific Considerations

#### SaaS:
- Focus on MRR, ARR, churn, LTV:CAC
- Show product-market fit
- Emphasize recurring revenue
- Highlight scalability

#### Marketplace:
- Demonstrate liquidity
- Show supply and demand growth
- Explain chicken-and-egg solution
- Network effects

#### Consumer:
- Show user growth and engagement
- Viral coefficients
- Path to monetization
- Retention metrics

#### Hardware:
- Manufacturing and supply chain
- Margins and costs
- IP and patents
- Distribution strategy

#### Biotech/Healthcare:
- Clinical data and trials
- Regulatory pathway
- IP strategy
- Market access

### 10. Investor-Specific Customization

Research and tailor for:
- Investor thesis and focus areas
- Portfolio companies (synergies)
- Check size and stage
- Value-add beyond capital
- Geographic focus
- Industry expertise

### 11. Metrics That Matter by Stage

#### Pre-Seed/Seed:
- Problem validation
- Early traction or LOIs
- Team pedigree
- Market insight

#### Series A:
- Product-market fit
- $1M+ ARR
- Clear unit economics
- Repeatable sales process

#### Series B+:
- Proven growth
- $10M+ ARR
- Path to profitability
- Market leadership potential

## Output Format

Provide:

1. **Complete Slide-by-Slide Outline:**
   - Slide title
   - Key messages (3-5 bullets)
   - Supporting data points
   - Visual description
   - Speaker notes

2. **Full Content for Each Slide:**
   - Headlines
   - Body copy
   - Data to include
   - Visuals to create

3. **One-Page Executive Summary:**
   - Company overview
   - Problem and solution
   - Market opportunity
   - Traction
   - Team
   - Ask

4. **Presentation Script:**
   - What to say for each slide (1-2 minutes per slide)
   - Transitions between slides
   - Key emphasis points

5. **Design Specifications:**
   - Color palette
   - Typography
   - Layout templates
   - Image style guide

6. **Q&A Preparation:**
   - 20-30 likely questions with answers

Customize the deck based on:
- Audience type
- Presentation context
- Stage of company
- Industry

Ask clarifying questions about the business, audience, stage, funding goals, and any existing materials before creating the pitch deck.
