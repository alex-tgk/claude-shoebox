# User Interview Questions & Script Generator

Generate comprehensive user interview questions and scripts for product research and customer discovery.

## Instructions

Create detailed user interview materials including:

### 1. Interview Overview
- Research objectives
- Key questions to answer
- Interview type (discovery, validation, usability, etc.)
- Target participants
- Interview duration (typically 30-60 minutes)
- Number of interviews planned

### 2. Participant Screening

#### Screening Criteria:
- Demographics (age, location, etc.)
- Role/job title
- Industry/company size
- Current behaviors or tool usage
- Pain points or needs
- Disqualifying factors

#### Screening Questions:
- 5-7 questions to identify right participants
- Include mix of closed and open-ended questions
- Qualification logic (must have/nice to have)
- Thank you/rejection messages

#### Recruiting Strategy:
- Where to find participants
- Outreach message templates
- Incentive structure
- Scheduling process

### 3. Interview Script Structure

#### Introduction (5 minutes):
- Welcome and thank you
- Explain purpose of the interview
- Set expectations (time, format, recording)
- Establish rapport
- Obtain consent for recording
- Explain confidentiality
- Encourage honesty and openness
- "There are no wrong answers"

#### Warm-up Questions (5 minutes):
- Easy, non-threatening questions
- Build comfort and trust
- Gather basic context
- Example questions provided

#### Main Interview Sections (35-40 minutes):
- Background and context
- Current state and behaviors
- Pain points and challenges
- Goals and desires
- Solution exploration
- Feature reactions
- Buying process

#### Closing (5-10 minutes):
- Summarize key takeaways
- Ask for clarification
- Open-ended "anything else"
- Thank participant
- Explain next steps
- Request referrals

### 4. Interview Question Bank

#### Background and Context Questions:
- "Tell me about your role and what you do day-to-day."
- "Walk me through a typical day/week for you."
- "How long have you been in this role/industry?"
- "What does your team structure look like?"
- "What are your primary responsibilities?"
- "What does success look like in your role?"

#### Current State and Behavior Questions:
- "How do you currently handle [problem/task]?"
- "Walk me through the last time you [did relevant action]."
- "What tools do you currently use for [task]?"
- "How often do you [perform specific behavior]?"
- "Who else is involved in this process?"
- "What does your workflow look like?"

#### Pain Points and Challenges Questions:
- "What's most frustrating about [current process]?"
- "What takes up the most time in your workflow?"
- "Where do things typically go wrong?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would you change?"
- "What keeps you up at night related to [topic]?"
- "What's the biggest bottleneck in your process?"
- "Tell me about a time when [problem] caused significant issues."

#### Goals and Motivations Questions:
- "What are you trying to achieve?"
- "What would make you more successful?"
- "How do you measure success for [task]?"
- "What are your goals for the next quarter/year?"
- "What metrics do you care most about?"
- "What would make your job easier?"

#### Solution Exploration Questions:
- "What have you tried to solve this problem?"
- "What worked and what didn't?"
- "What would an ideal solution look like?"
- "What features would be must-haves vs. nice-to-haves?"
- "How would you want this to work?"
- "What would make you switch from your current solution?"

#### Product/Concept Reaction Questions:
- "What's your first impression?"
- "How would you use this?"
- "What stands out to you?"
- "What's confusing or unclear?"
- "What's missing that you'd expect to see?"
- "How does this compare to what you use now?"
- "Would you use this? Why or why not?"

#### Buying Process Questions:
- "How do you typically evaluate and purchase tools?"
- "Who else is involved in the decision?"
- "What's your budget approval process?"
- "How long does a typical buying cycle take?"
- "What are your evaluation criteria?"
- "What would need to be true for you to buy this?"
- "What concerns would you have before purchasing?"

#### Prioritization Questions:
- "If you could only have one feature, what would it be?"
- "How would you prioritize these options?"
- "What would you be willing to pay for this?"
- "What would you NOT be willing to pay for?"

### 5. Interview Best Practices

#### Do's:
- Ask open-ended questions ("How...?" "Why...?" "Tell me about...")
- Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
- Dig deeper with follow-ups ("Can you tell me more about that?")
- Ask for specific examples ("Tell me about the last time...")
- Embrace silence (let them think)
- Take detailed notes or record
- Watch for body language (if in-person/video)
- Stay curious and probe assumptions
- Validate their experiences
- Be empathetic

#### Don'ts:
- Don't ask leading questions
- Don't pitch or sell
- Don't interrupt
- Don't make assumptions
- Don't skip follow-up questions
- Don't take everything at face value
- Don't forget to ask "why"
- Don't multitask during the interview
- Don't jump to conclusions

#### Follow-up Probes:
- "Can you tell me more about that?"
- "Why is that important to you?"
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "What happened next?"
- "Can you give me a specific example?"
- "Why do you think that is?"
- "What do you mean by [term they used]?"
- "Help me understand..."

### 6. Interview Types and Customizations

#### Problem Discovery Interviews:
- Focus on understanding problems and context
- Minimal product discussion
- Deep dive into current state
- Pain point exploration

#### Solution Validation Interviews:
- Test hypotheses about solutions
- Show mockups or prototypes
- Gather reactions and feedback
- Understand willingness to pay

#### Usability Testing Interviews:
- Task-based observation
- Think-aloud protocol
- Identify friction points
- Measure task completion

#### Jobs-to-be-Done Interviews:
- Focus on the "job" they're hiring a product for
- Timeline of first thought to purchase
- Forces pushing and pulling decisions
- Anxiety and habits

### 7. Interview Segments by Persona

Create customized scripts for:
- End users
- Decision makers
- Technical evaluators
- Champions/advocates
- Economic buyers

Tailor questions to:
- Their specific role and concerns
- Their influence in the process
- Their pain points
- Their success metrics

### 8. Remote Interview Setup

#### Technical Preparation:
- Video conferencing tool (Zoom, Google Meet)
- Recording setup and permissions
- Screen sharing capability
- Backup communication method
- Note-taking system

#### Interview Etiquette:
- Test technology beforehand
- Send calendar invite with clear instructions
- Join 5 minutes early
- Have backup plan for tech issues
- Follow up with thank you email

### 9. Note-Taking Template

Structure for capturing insights:
- Date and participant ID
- Key quotes (verbatim)
- Pain points observed
- Goals mentioned
- Current solutions
- Feature reactions
- Surprising insights
- Follow-up questions
- Researcher observations

### 10. Analysis Framework

#### After Each Interview:
- Immediate debrief and notes review
- Highlight key insights
- Tag themes and patterns
- Update research questions
- Identify quotes for sharing

#### After All Interviews:
- Affinity mapping of insights
- Pattern identification across interviews
- Persona validation or creation
- Key themes and findings
- Recommendations and next steps
- Highlight reel of best quotes

### 11. Insight Synthesis

Organize findings by:
- Pain points (frequency, intensity)
- Goals and motivations
- Current solutions and workarounds
- Feature desires (must-have vs. nice-to-have)
- Buying process and objections
- Unexpected insights
- Quotes that tell the story

### 12. Reporting Template

#### Research Report Structure:
- Executive summary
- Research objectives and methodology
- Participant overview
- Key findings (3-5 major insights)
- Supporting evidence and quotes
- Recommendations
- Next steps

#### Presentation Format:
- Problem statements
- User journey insights
- Opportunity areas
- Feature prioritization
- Design implications
- Go-to-market implications

### 13. Consent and Ethics

#### Consent Form Elements:
- Purpose of research
- How data will be used
- Recording permissions
- Anonymity and confidentiality
- Right to withdraw
- Contact information
- Signature/verbal consent

#### Ethical Guidelines:
- Respect participant time
- Protect privacy
- Use insights responsibly
- Compensate fairly
- Be transparent about purpose
- Store data securely

### 14. Participant Incentives

Recommendations for:
- B2C participants ($50-100 gift cards)
- B2B participants ($100-200 or more for executives)
- Alternative incentives (donation, product credit)
- When to send incentives
- Thank you message

### 15. Follow-up Process

#### Post-Interview:
- Send thank you email within 24 hours
- Share any promised resources
- Send incentive/compensation
- Request permission to follow up
- Ask for referrals to similar participants

#### Ongoing Relationship:
- Add to research panel
- Invite to beta testing
- Share relevant updates
- Request future interviews

## Output Format

Provide:

1. **Complete Interview Script:**
   - Introduction verbatim
   - All questions organized by section
   - Estimated time per section
   - Transition statements
   - Closing statements

2. **Screening Survey:**
   - 5-7 screening questions
   - Qualification logic
   - Recruitment email template

3. **Question Bank:**
   - 50-75 questions organized by category
   - Customized for the specific research goal
   - Follow-up probes for each main question

4. **Note-Taking Template:**
   - Structured format for capturing insights
   - Key areas to observe

5. **Analysis Framework:**
   - How to synthesize findings
   - Reporting template

6. **Checklists:**
   - Pre-interview preparation
   - During interview best practices
   - Post-interview follow-up

Include specific questions tailored to the product, industry, and research objectives.

Ask clarifying questions about research goals, target participants, product stage, and specific hypotheses to test before generating the interview materials.
