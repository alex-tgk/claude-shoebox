# Revenue Sharing & Economics

## Revenue Model Structure

### Commission Structure

**Joint Sales (Both companies involved):**
```
Scenario: Customer purchases both products

Option A - Revenue Split:
• [Your Company] keeps: 100% of [Product] revenue
• [Partner Company] keeps: 100% of [Partner Product] revenue
• Both pay marketplace/referral fee to initiating party

Option B - Commission on Partner Product:
• [Your Company] receives: 20% commission on [Partner Product] revenue
• [Partner Company] receives: 20% commission on [Your Product] revenue
```

**Referral Sales (One company refers):**
```
Referral from [Your Company] to [Partner]:
• [Partner] pays [Your Company]: 15% commission on first-year revenue
• Recurring commission: 10% on renewal revenue (Years 2-3)

Referral from [Partner] to [Your Company]:
• [Your Company] pays [Partner]: 15% commission on first-year revenue
• Recurring commission: 10% on renewal revenue (Years 2-3)
```

**Marketplace Sales:**
```
Customer discovers via [Partner] Marketplace:
• Customer pays [Partner Company]
• [Partner Company] keeps: 25% marketplace fee
• [Partner Company] pays [Your Company]: 75% of list price
• [Your Company] receives payment net-30
```

### Deal Registration System

**Protected Deals:**
• Partner identifies opportunity first → Gets commission
• Logged in deal registration system
• 30-day protection period
• Conflicts resolved by partnership committee

### Revenue Projections

**Year 1 Financial Model:**
