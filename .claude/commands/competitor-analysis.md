# Competitor Analysis & Comparison Matrix

Analyze competitors and create a comprehensive comparison matrix for the product or market specified by the user.

## Instructions

Conduct a detailed competitive analysis including:

### 1. Competitor Identification
- Direct competitors (same product/service)
- Indirect competitors (alternative solutions)
- Potential future competitors
- Market leaders vs. emerging players

### 2. Company Profiles
For each major competitor, analyze:
- Company background and history
- Size and scale (employees, revenue, funding)
- Geographic presence
- Target market and positioning
- Business model

### 3. Product/Service Analysis
- Core features and capabilities
- Product strengths and weaknesses
- Technology stack and infrastructure
- User experience and design
- Innovation and R&D focus

### 4. Market Positioning
- Brand positioning and messaging
- Value proposition
- Differentiation strategy
- Target customer segments
- Pricing strategy

### 5. Go-to-Market Strategy
- Marketing channels and tactics
- Sales approach (direct, channel, self-service)
- Customer acquisition strategy
- Partnership ecosystem

### 6. Performance Metrics
- Market share
- Customer base size
- Growth rate
- Customer satisfaction scores
- Online presence and engagement

### 7. Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats (SWOT)
For each major competitor:
- Key strengths
- Notable weaknesses
- Growth opportunities they're pursuing
- Threats to their position

### 8. Competitive Comparison Matrix
Create a detailed matrix comparing:
- Features and capabilities
- Pricing tiers
- Target audience
- Integrations
- Support options
- User ratings/reviews
- Unique selling points

### 9. Competitive Advantages & Gaps
- What competitors do well
- Where competitors fall short
- Underserved market segments
- Opportunities for differentiation

### 10. Competitive Strategy Recommendations
- How to position against competitors
- Unique value propositions to emphasize
- Market gaps to exploit
- Competitive moats to build

### 11. Competitive Intelligence Sources
- Where to monitor competitor activity
- Key metrics to track ongoing
- Alert systems to implement

## Output Format

Provide:
1. Executive summary of competitive landscape
2. Individual competitor profiles (top 3-5)
3. Comparison matrix in table format
4. Visual competitive positioning map description
5. Strategic recommendations
6. Action items for competitive advantage

Ask for details about the product, industry, and specific competitors to analyze before proceeding.
