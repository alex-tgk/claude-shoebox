# Video Sales Letter (VSL) Creator

Create a complete video sales letter package with script, storyboard, landing page, and follow-up system for the product or service specified by the user.

## Instructions

Generate a comprehensive VSL campaign including:

### 1. VSL Strategy Overview
- Product/service being sold
- Target audience and pain points
- Primary offer and pricing
- Sales goal (conversions, revenue targets)
- Video length (typically 10-15 minutes)
- Distribution channels (landing page, ads, email)
- Success metrics

### 2. VSL Framework Selection

Choose and apply proven frameworks:

#### PAS Framework (Problem-Agitate-Solution):
- **Problem:** Identify the core problem (2-3 minutes)
- **Agitate:** Make the pain vivid and urgent (2-3 minutes)
- **Solution:** Present your offer as the answer (6-10 minutes)

#### AIDA Framework:
- **Attention:** Hook with compelling opening (30 seconds)
- **Interest:** Build curiosity and engagement (2-3 minutes)
- **Desire:** Create want through benefits (4-6 minutes)
- **Action:** Drive to purchase decision (2-3 minutes)

#### Star-Story-Solution Framework:
- **Star:** Introduce relatable character (1-2 minutes)
- **Story:** Share transformation journey (3-5 minutes)
- **Solution:** Reveal the method/product (5-8 minutes)

### 3. Complete VSL Script (10-15 minutes)

#### Opening Hook (0:00-0:30)
**Purpose:** Stop the scroll, grab attention immediately

**Elements:**
- Pattern interrupt (bold statement, question, or statistic)
- Promise of value to come
- Curiosity gap creation
- Relatability establishment

**Example Hooks:**
- "What if I told you [bold claim]?"
- "In the next 12 minutes, I'm going to show you..."
- "WARNING: If you're [target audience], don't close this page..."
- "Imagine [desired outcome]..."

**Script Template:**
```
[BOLD STATEMENT or QUESTION]

If you're [target audience] who's struggling with [problem],
what I'm about to share with you could [transformation promise].

In fact, in the next [X] minutes, I'm going to reveal [specific promise]
that has helped [social proof number] people [achieve result].

But first, let me ask you something...
```

---

#### Problem Identification (0:30-3:00)
**Purpose:** Make viewer feel understood, establish credibility

**Elements:**
- List 5-7 specific pain points
- "You might have tried..." (failed solutions)
- Current frustrations and costs
- Emotional impact of the problem
- Why existing solutions don't work

**Script Template:**
```
Does any of this sound familiar?

[Pain point 1 - specific scenario]
[Pain point 2 - specific scenario]
[Pain point 3 - specific scenario]

Maybe you've tried [failed solution 1], [failed solution 2], or [failed solution 3].

But nothing seems to work because [root cause].

And every day this problem continues, it's costing you [tangible cost]...
```

---

#### Agitation (3:00-5:00)
**Purpose:** Intensify the pain, create urgency

**Elements:**
- Future projection (what happens if nothing changes)
- Opportunity cost (what you're missing out on)
- Emotional consequences
- Time sensitivity
- Comparison to others who've solved it

**Script Template:**
```
Here's the harsh truth...

If you don't solve this soon, [negative future outcome].

While you're stuck dealing with [problem], your competitors/peers are [success others are having].

Each day that goes by, you're losing [specific loss].

But it doesn't have to be this way...
```

---

#### Solution Introduction (5:00-6:30)
**Purpose:** Present your product as the answer

**Elements:**
- The discovery moment
- What makes this different
- Why it works when others fail
- Unique mechanism or methodology
- Origin story (briefly)

**Script Template:**
```
That's exactly why I created [Product Name].

After [years/experience] of [background], I discovered that [key insight].

This led to a completely different approach...

Instead of [old way], [Product Name] uses [unique mechanism] to [deliver result].

This is the same [method/system/product] that [social proof].

Here's how it works...
```

---

#### How It Works (6:30-8:30)
**Purpose:** Explain the mechanism and build belief

**Elements:**
- 3-step process or key features
- Why each part matters
- Proof points for each claim
- Address skepticism proactively
- Show ease of use

**Script Template:**
```
[Product Name] works through a simple 3-step process:

STEP 1: [Action]
[Benefit and why it matters]
[Proof or testimonial]

STEP 2: [Action]
[Benefit and why it matters]
[Proof or testimonial]

STEP 3: [Action]
[Benefit and why it matters]
[Proof or testimonial]

And the best part? You don't need to [remove friction points].
```

---

#### Social Proof & Results (8:30-10:00)
**Purpose:** Build credibility and show results

**Elements:**
- Specific customer results (3-5 examples)
- Before/after comparisons
- Testimonials (video clips or text)
- Statistics and data
- Credentials or media mentions

**Script Template:**
```
Don't just take my word for it...

[Customer Name] used [Product] and [specific result with numbers].
"[Testimonial quote]"

[Customer Name] went from [before] to [after] in just [timeframe].
"[Testimonial quote]"

In total, [Product] has helped [number] people [achieve result],
with an average of [impressive statistic].

[Media mentions, awards, or credentials]
```

---

#### The Offer (10:00-12:00)
**Purpose:** Present the complete package with value stacking

**Elements:**
- Main product/service
- Bonuses (3-5 bonuses)
- Total value calculation
- Actual price reveal
- Price justification
- Guarantee
- Scarcity/urgency element

**Script Template:**
```
So here's everything you're getting today:

First, you get [Main Product] - normally [higher value], but today [price]

PLUS, you're also getting these exclusive bonuses:

BONUS #1: [Bonus Name] (Value: $X)
[What it is and why it matters]

BONUS #2: [Bonus Name] (Value: $X)
[What it is and why it matters]

BONUS #3: [Bonus Name] (Value: $X)
[What it is and why it matters]

That's a total value of $[Total Value].

But you're not going to pay $[Total Value]...
You're not even going to pay $[Mid-tier anchor]...

When you act today, you'll get everything for just [Final Price].

That's [percentage]% off the regular price.

But that's not all...
```

---

#### Guarantee (12:00-12:30)
**Purpose:** Remove risk and objections

**Elements:**
- Specific guarantee terms
- Risk reversal language
- Why you can offer this guarantee
- What happens if they're not satisfied
- Make it sound generous

**Script Template:**
```
And to make this a complete no-brainer, I'm including my
[30/60/90]-Day [Type] Guarantee.

Here's how it works:

[Detailed guarantee terms - make it strong]

So you literally cannot lose. Either [Product] delivers [result],
or you pay nothing.

The only way you can lose is by not trying this at all.

Why can I offer such a strong guarantee?

Because I've seen [Product] work for [number] people,
and I'm confident it will work for you too.
```

---

#### Call to Action (12:30-14:00)
**Purpose:** Drive immediate action

**Elements:**
- Clear instructions (click button, fill out form)
- Urgency reminder (limited time/quantity)
- Recap of what they're getting
- What happens after they order
- Final emotional appeal
- Contrast (what if you don't act)

**Script Template:**
```
So here's what to do next:

Simply click the button below this video right now.

You'll be taken to a secure order form where you can get
instant access to [Product] and all the bonuses.

Remember, this special price of [Price] is only available [urgency element].

After [deadline/quantity], the price goes up to [regular price],
or this offer comes down entirely.

So click the button now and get started today.

In just a few minutes, you could be [experiencing benefit]...

Or you can keep struggling with [problem].

The choice is yours.

But if you're serious about [goal], click the button below now.

I'll see you on the inside.
```

---

#### Alternative: FAQ Section (Optional, 14:00-15:00)
**Purpose:** Handle remaining objections

**Common Questions:**
- How long does it take to see results?
- Do I need any special skills/equipment?
- What if I've tried everything else?
- Is this really for me?
- What kind of support do I get?
- Can I get a refund?

### 4. Slide-by-Slide Storyboard

For each 30-second segment, specify:

#### Visual Elements:
- Camera angle (talking head, screen recording, b-roll)
- Text overlays (bullet points, stats)
- Graphics or animations
- Product demonstrations
- Testimonial displays

#### Example Storyboard Format:
```
SLIDE 1 (0:00-0:30) - Opening Hook
Visuals: Close-up of presenter, high energy
Text Overlay: "[Bold statement]"
B-roll: [Quick cuts of results/success]

SLIDE 2 (0:30-1:00) - Problem Introduction
Visuals: Split screen - problem scenarios
Text Overlay: Pain points appear as bullet points
Graphics: Check marks next to each pain point

[Continue for full video length...]
```

### 5. Visual Direction & B-Roll Suggestions

#### Production Style:
- High energy talking head
- Professional but authentic
- Screen recordings for demonstrations
- Customer testimonial clips
- Stock footage for emotional moments
- Animated graphics for statistics

#### B-Roll Categories:
**Problem Scenes:**
- Frustrated person at desk
- Clock ticking (time waste)
- Stress and overwhelm visuals
- Failed attempts

**Solution Scenes:**
- Product in action
- Happy users
- Results being achieved
- Ease of use demonstrations

**Lifestyle Scenes:**
- Before transformation (struggle)
- After transformation (success)
- Aspirational imagery
- Social proof moments

#### Text Overlay Strategy:
- Key statistics (large, bold)
- Pain points (bullet format)
- Benefits (check marks)
- Price reveals (attention-grabbing)
- Guarantee terms (trust-building)
- CTA instructions (clear, directive)

### 6. VSL Landing Page

#### Page Structure:

**Above the Fold:**
- Attention-grabbing headline
- Subheadline with benefit
- VSL video player (auto-play optional)
- "Click to unmute" overlay if auto-playing

**Below Video:**
- Primary CTA button (large, contrasting color)
- Trust indicators (secure payment, guarantee badge)
- Urgency indicator (countdown timer, limited spots)

**Supporting Elements:**
- Bullet points of what they'll learn
- Testimonial sidebar
- Progress indicator ("85% watched this video")
- Exit-intent popup

**Bottom of Page:**
- Additional CTA
- FAQ accordion
- Final testimonials
- Footer (privacy, terms, contact)

#### Landing Page Copy:

**Headline Options:**
- "Discover The [Adjective] Way To [Achieve Result] Without [Pain Point]"
- "How [Target Audience] Are [Achieving Result] In Record Time"
- "The Secret To [Desired Outcome] Finally Revealed"

**Subheadline Template:**
```
Watch this free video to discover the [method/system] that's helped
[number] [target audience] [achieve specific result] in just [timeframe]...
even if you've [tried everything else].
```

**CTA Button Text:**
- "Yes! Give Me Access Now"
- "Get Instant Access"
- "Claim My [Product] Today"
- "Start Your Transformation"

#### Technical Specifications:
- Video player: Vimeo or Wistia (tracking enabled)
- Mobile responsive design
- Fast loading (optimized video)
- No navigation menu (single purpose)
- Scroll tracking enabled
- Heat mapping installed

### 7. Follow-Up Email Sequence

#### Email 1: Immediate (Watched 75%+, Didn't Buy)
**Subject:** "Did you have a question about [Product]?"
**Content:**
- Noticed they watched the video
- Ask if they have questions
- Address common objection
- Remind of guarantee
- CTA to purchase

#### Email 2: Day 1 (Abandoned Cart)
**Subject:** "You left something behind..."
**Content:**
- Cart reminder
- What they're missing out on
- Urgency reminder (time/quantity)
- Additional testimonial
- CTA with easy checkout

#### Email 3: Day 2 (FAQ Focused)
**Subject:** "Quick question about [Product]..."
**Content:**
- Answer top objection/question
- Case study or result story
- Simplified explanation
- Risk reversal emphasis
- CTA

#### Email 4: Day 3 (Deadline Warning)
**Subject:** "Only [X] hours left..."
**Content:**
- Urgent deadline reminder
- What they lose if they don't act
- Scarcity element (spots, bonuses)
- Strong CTA
- Final chance language

#### Email 5: Day 4 (Social Proof)
**Subject:** "This just in from [Customer Name]..."
**Content:**
- Fresh testimonial or result
- Before/after story
- "Others like you" angle
- Guarantee reminder
- Last chance CTA

#### Email 6: Day 5 (Takeaway)
**Subject:** "We're closing the doors..."
**Content:**
- Final notice
- Offer ending
- What happens next (higher price, no bonuses)
- Emotional appeal
- Emergency CTA

#### Email 7: Day 7 (Re-engagement)
**Subject:** "Can I ask you something?"
**Content:**
- Feedback request (why didn't you buy?)
- Different angle or alternative offer
- Stay connected opportunity
- Nurture continuation

### 8. Analytics & Tracking Setup

#### Video Analytics:
**Track:**
- Play rate (impressions vs plays)
- Engagement (average watch time)
- Drop-off points (where viewers leave)
- Replay rate
- Watch completion rate (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%)

**Tools:**
- Wistia, Vimeo Pro, or YouTube Analytics
- Google Analytics 4 events
- Hotjar session recordings

#### Conversion Tracking:
**Metrics:**
- VSL view → click-through rate
- Click → checkout started
- Checkout → purchase completion
- Overall conversion rate
- Revenue per video view
- Cost per acquisition (if running ads)

**Tag Setup:**
- Facebook Pixel events (ViewContent, AddToCart, Purchase)
- Google Ads conversion tracking
- TikTok Pixel (if applicable)
- Custom event triggers

#### Attribution:
- UTM parameters for traffic sources
- Affiliate tracking (if applicable)
- Email click tracking
- Split test tracking

### 9. A/B Testing Strategy

#### Test Variables:

**Headlines (Landing Page):**
- Benefit-driven vs curiosity-driven
- Short vs long
- Question vs statement
- With/without numbers

**VSL Variations:**
- Different hooks (first 30 seconds)
- Script length (10 min vs 15 min)
- CTA frequency (1 vs multiple)
- Testimonial placement

**Offer Presentation:**
- Price reveal timing
- Bonus order/quantity
- Guarantee terms strength
- Scarcity type (time vs quantity)

**CTA Buttons:**
- Button copy ("Buy Now" vs "Get Access")
- Button color
- Button size/placement
- Button frequency on page

**Email Sequence:**
- Subject line variations
- Send timing
- Email quantity
- Urgency level

#### Testing Protocol:
1. Baseline performance (1 week)
2. One variable at a time
3. Statistical significance (95%+ confidence)
4. Minimum sample size: 100 conversions per variant
5. Winner implementation
6. Continuous optimization

## Output Format

Provide:

1. **Complete VSL Script** (word-for-word, time-stamped)
2. **Detailed Storyboard** (slide-by-slide visual direction)
3. **B-Roll Shot List** (30+ specific b-roll suggestions)
4. **Landing Page Copy** (complete page content with HTML structure)
5. **7-Email Follow-Up Sequence** (complete emails with subject lines)
6. **Analytics Dashboard Template** (key metrics to track)
7. **A/B Testing Plan** (5+ test variations with hypotheses)
8. **Production Timeline** (from script to launch)

Format the script with:
- Time stamps every 30 seconds
- Visual cues [in brackets]
- EMPHASIS indicators in caps
- (Pause) notations
- {Text overlay content}

Ask clarifying questions about the product, target audience, price point, existing assets, and unique selling propositions before creating the VSL.
