# Lead Magnet Generator

Create a complete lead generation system with high-value downloadable asset, landing page, delivery automation, and nurture sequence for the product or service specified by the user.

## Instructions

Generate a comprehensive lead magnet package including:

### 1. Lead Magnet Strategy

#### Asset Type Selection:

**Educational Content:**
- Ebook (15-30 pages)
- Guide or whitepaper
- Report or case study
- Workbook or playbook
- Checklist or cheat sheet
- Template or toolkit
- Swipe file
- Resource list

**Interactive Tools:**
- Calculator or assessment
- Quiz or survey
- Spreadsheet tool
- Notion template
- Figma template

**Media Assets:**
- Video training series
- Audio course or podcast series
- Webinar recording
- Interview compilation

**Criteria for Selection:**
- Quick to consume (or implement)
- High perceived value
- Solves specific pain point
- Related to paid offering
- Easy to deliver digitally
- Shareable format

#### Lead Magnet Attributes:

**S.P.E.C.I.F.I.C. Framework:**
- **Specific:** Solves one clear problem
- **Promising:** Delivers concrete outcome
- **Easy:** Simple to consume and implement
- **Consumable:** Can be finished quickly (10-15 min)
- **Immediate:** Provides instant value
- **Formatted:** Professional design
- **I**mpactful: Creates transformation
- **Credible:** Backed by expertise

### 2. Content Creation by Type

#### A. Ebook/Guide Format

**Structure (20-25 pages):**

**Cover Page:**
- Eye-catching title
- Compelling subtitle
- Author name/brand
- Professional design

**Page 2: Copyright & Disclaimer**

**Page 3: Table of Contents**

**Page 4: Introduction (2 pages)**
```
- Hook: Start with relatable problem
- Why this matters (statistics, impact)
- What they'll learn/gain
- How to use this guide
- Author credibility
```

**Pages 6-20: Core Content (5-7 chapters)**
```
Chapter Format:
• Chapter Title (problem or promise)
• Introduction (why this matters)
• Main content (3-5 key points)
• Examples or case studies
• Action steps
• Key takeaway box

Design Elements:
- Bullet points for scannability
- Bold key phrases
- Highlighted quotes or stats
- Icons or simple graphics
- Plenty of white space
- Consistent formatting
```

**Sample Chapter Template:**
```
CHAPTER 3: [Clear Benefit or How-To]

You're about to discover [promise].

The Problem with [Old Way]:
[Explain common mistake or challenge]

The Solution:
[Your method or framework]

Here's How It Works:

STEP 1: [Action]
[Explanation and why it matters]
[Example or mini case study]

STEP 2: [Action]
[Explanation and why it matters]
[Example or mini case study]

STEP 3: [Action]
[Explanation and why it matters]
[Example or mini case study]

ACTION ITEM: [Specific task to implement this]

KEY TAKEAWAY: [One sentence summary]
```

**Page 21: Conclusion**
```
- Recap main points
- Encouragement
- Next steps
- Call-to-action (soft pitch)
```

**Page 22: Resources**
- Further reading
- Tools mentioned
- Links to your content

**Page 23: About the Author**
- Bio
- Photo
- Credentials
- Social links
- Website

**Page 24: Your Offer (CTA)**
```
"Ready to Take This Further?"

[Your Paid Product/Service]
[Key benefits - 3-5 bullets]
[Special offer or bonus]
[Button: Learn More →]
```

**Page 25: Back Cover**
- Summary of value delivered
- Brand logo
- Website URL

### 3. Design & Formatting

#### Design Principles:

**Visual Hierarchy:**
- Clear headlines (large, bold)
- Subheadings (medium weight)
- Body text (readable size, 12-14pt)
- Ample white space
- Consistent margins

**Color Scheme:**
- 2-3 brand colors
- High contrast for readability
- Use color for emphasis (sparingly)
- Professional palette

**Typography:**
- Sans-serif for headers (modern, clean)
- Serif or sans-serif for body (readable)
- Maximum 2-3 fonts
- Consistent hierarchy

**Graphics:**
- Custom icons (not overused stock)
- Simple illustrations
- Photos (high quality, relevant)
- Charts or infographics (for data)
- Consistent style throughout

**Branding:**
- Logo on cover and footer
- Brand colors throughout
- Consistent font usage
- Professional appearance
- Cohesive with website design

#### Tools for Creation:

**Design Software:**
- Canva (easiest, templates available)
- Adobe InDesign (professional)
- Figma (collaborative)
- Pages/Word (basic)
- Google Slides (surprisingly effective)

**Templates Sources:**
- Canva templates (search "ebook" or "workbook")
- Creative Market (paid templates)
- Etsy (affordable templates)
- Designrr (create from blog posts)

**Technical Setup:**
- Trigger: Form submission

- Plain text or simple HTML

### 8. Analytics Tracking

#### Metrics to Track:

**Landing Page:**
- Page views
- Unique visitors
- Traffic sources
- Conversion rate (opt-ins / visitors)
- Time on page
- Scroll depth
- Button clicks
- Exit rate
- Mobile vs desktop

**Email Performance:**
- Delivery rate

- Click-through rate (benchmark: 2-5%)
- Download/conversion rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Reply rate
- Forwarding rate

**Lead Quality:**
- Email engagement score
- Content consumption
- Link clicks
- Replies and interactions
- Purchase rate
- Time to purchase

#### Tracking Setup:

**Landing Page Tools:**
- Google Analytics 4
- Facebook Pixel
- Hotjar (heat maps)
- Clarity (session recordings)
- UTM parameters for traffic sources

**Email Tools:**
- Email service provider analytics (ConvertKit, MailerLite, etc.)
- Link tracking (Bitly, custom UTMs)
- Custom conversion events

**Goals to Set:**
- Opt-in completion
- Download/access
- Email opens (sequence)
- Link clicks (sequence)
- Purchase conversion

### 10. A/B Testing Plan

#### Elements to Test:

**Landing Page:**
- Headline variations (3-5 versions)
- Subheadline
- CTA button text ("Download" vs "Get Access" vs "Send It To Me")
- Button color
- Form length (email only vs name + email)
- Page length (short vs long)
- Social proof placement
- Hero image vs video

**Lead Magnet:**
- Title variations
- Format (ebook vs checklist vs video)
- Length (short vs comprehensive)
- Topic angle
- Cover design

**Email:**
- Subject lines (test every email)
- Preview text
- Email length
- CTA placement
- Plain text vs HTML
- Personalization

**Ads:**
- Headlines (5+ variations)
- Primary text
- Images/videos (10+ variations)
- Audiences
- Placements
- Call-to-action button

## Output Format

Provide:

1. **Complete Lead Magnet Asset** (PDF, template files, or detailed content outline)
2. **Landing Page Copy** (full page with all sections)
3. **Landing Page Design** (wireframe or HTML/CSS)
4. **Thank You Page** (content and layout)
5. **Delivery Email** (subject and body)
6. **7-Email Nurture Sequence** (complete emails with subjects)
7. **Analytics Dashboard Template** (key metrics to track)
8. **Promotion Plan** (traffic sources and ad copy examples)
9. **A/B Testing Strategy** (5+ test variations)
10. **Implementation Checklist** (step-by-step setup guide)

Ask clarifying questions about the target audience, pain points, existing content, related paid offerings, email platform, budget, and goals before creating the lead magnet system.
