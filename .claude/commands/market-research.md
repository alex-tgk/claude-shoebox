# Market Research Analyzer

Conduct comprehensive market research for the product idea or business concept provided by the user.

## Instructions

Perform a thorough market analysis covering:

### 1. Market Overview
- Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- Market size estimates and growth projections
- Geographic considerations

### 2. Industry Analysis
- Industry trends and dynamics
- Market maturity stage
- Growth drivers and inhibitors
- Regulatory environment
- Technology trends affecting the market

### 3. Target Market Segmentation
- Customer segments
- Segment size and characteristics
- Priority segments
- Segment-specific needs and pain points

### 4. Customer Analysis
- Demographics and psychographics
- Buying behavior and patterns
- Decision-making process
- Budget considerations
- Current solutions being used

### 5. Competitive Landscape
- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Substitute products/services
- Market share distribution
- Competitive advantages and disadvantages

### 6. Market Needs and Gaps
- Unmet needs in the market
- Pain points not addressed by current solutions
- Opportunities for differentiation
- Innovation opportunities

### 7. Pricing Analysis
- Current market pricing ranges
- Price sensitivity analysis
- Value-based pricing opportunities
- Revenue models in use

### 8. Distribution Channels
- Common distribution channels
- Channel effectiveness
- Partnership opportunities
- Direct vs. indirect sales

### 9. Market Entry Barriers
- Capital requirements
- Technical barriers
- Regulatory barriers
- Brand loyalty and switching costs

### 10. Market Validation
- Evidence of demand
- Early adopter signals
- Market readiness assessment
- Go-to-market feasibility

### 11. Strategic Recommendations
- Market entry strategy
- Positioning recommendations
- Priority actions
- Risk considerations

## Output Format

Present findings in a professional market research report format with:
- Executive summary
- Data-driven insights with sources
- Visual descriptions (charts, graphs, tables)
- Clear recommendations
- Supporting evidence and reasoning

Ask clarifying questions about the product idea, target market, and geography before conducting the research.
