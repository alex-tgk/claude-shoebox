# Create Content Calendar

Generate a comprehensive 90-day content strategy with daily posts, platform-specific content, content briefs, SEO integration, and team collaboration workflow.

## Instructions

You are tasked with creating a COMPLETE, revenue-focused content calendar and strategy. This is a one-shot command that must produce a fully planned content strategy ready for immediate execution.

### Step 1: Gather Requirements

First, ask the user these essential questions:
1. **Business/Brand**: What's the business name and what do you offer?
2. **Target Audience**: Who is your ideal customer? (demographics, interests)
3. **Goals**: What do you want to achieve? (brand awareness, leads, sales, traffic)
4. **Platforms**: Which platforms? (blog, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube)
5. **Content Themes**: What topics/pillars should content cover?
6. **Resources**: Who will create content? (team size, skills)
7. **Budget**: Content creation budget per month?
8. **Competitors**: Who are your main competitors?

### Step 9: Team Workflow

```markdown
# Content Team Workflow

## Team Structure
- **Content Manager**: Strategy, planning, approval
- **Writers**: Blog posts, scripts, captions
- **Designers**: Graphics, videos, thumbnails
- **Social Media Manager**: Posting, engagement, community
- **SEO Specialist**: Keywords, optimization
- **Analyst**: Performance tracking, reporting

## Weekly Schedule

### Monday
- 9 AM: Weekly planning meeting
- Review last week's performance
- Assign tasks for the week
- Discuss upcoming content
- Deadline: Writer submits drafts

### Tuesday
- Review and edit draft content
- Designer creates graphics
- Deadline: First round edits complete

### Wednesday
- Final reviews and approvals
- Schedule social posts
- Prepare email newsletter
- Deadline: All content approved

### Thursday
- Publish blog posts
- Upload YouTube videos
- Schedule week's social content
- Deadline: Week's content live/scheduled

### Friday
- Engagement and community management
- Monitor performance
- Prepare next week's brief
- Weekly report due

- **Content Calendar**: Airtable/Asana

- **Design**: Canva/Figma
- **Social Scheduling**: Buffer/Hootsuite
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, native platform analytics
- **SEO**: SEMrush/Ahrefs

1. Writer creates draft → Saves in shared folder
2. Editor reviews → Makes suggestions
```
