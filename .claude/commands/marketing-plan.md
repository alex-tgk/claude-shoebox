# Comprehensive Marketing Strategy Generator

Generate a complete marketing strategy and plan for the product, service, or campaign specified by the user.

## Instructions

Create a detailed marketing plan covering:

### 1. Executive Summary
- Marketing objectives
- Target audience overview
- Key strategies
- Budget summary
- Expected outcomes

### 2. Situation Analysis
- Current market position
- SWOT analysis
- Competitive landscape
- Market trends and opportunities
- Brand health assessment

### 3. Marketing Objectives
- Specific, measurable goals (SMART)
- Awareness goals
- Lead generation targets
- Conversion targets
- Revenue targets
- Customer retention goals
- Timeline for each objective

### 4. Target Audience
- Primary and secondary audiences
- Buyer personas summary
- Customer journey stages
- Audience insights and behaviors
- Segmentation strategy

### 5. Positioning and Messaging
- Brand positioning statement
- Value proposition
- Key messages by audience segment
- Unique selling propositions
- Brand voice and tone guidelines
- Messaging hierarchy

### 6. Marketing Strategy
- Overall strategic approach
- Market penetration strategy
- Growth strategy
- Positioning strategy
- Differentiation strategy

### 7. Marketing Mix (7Ps)
- Product strategy
- Pricing strategy
- Place (distribution) strategy
- Promotion strategy
- People strategy
- Process strategy
- Physical evidence strategy

### 8. Channel Strategy

#### Digital Channels:
- Website and landing pages
- SEO and content marketing
- Paid search (Google Ads)
- Paid social advertising
- Email marketing
- Social media organic
- Influencer marketing
- Affiliate marketing
- Display advertising
- Video marketing (YouTube, etc.)

#### Traditional Channels:
- Print advertising
- Direct mail
- Events and trade shows
- PR and media relations
- Partnerships
- Out-of-home advertising

For each channel:
- Objectives
- Target audience
- Key tactics
- Budget allocation
- Success metrics

### 9. Content Strategy
- Content pillars and themes
- Content types and formats
- Content calendar overview
- Distribution strategy
- Content promotion plan
- Repurposing strategy

### 10. Campaign Planning
- Campaign themes and concepts
- Campaign timeline
- Integrated campaign approach
- Creative requirements
- Launch strategy

### 11. Budget Allocation
- Total marketing budget
- Budget by channel
- Budget by campaign
- Fixed vs. variable costs
- ROI projections
- Resource requirements

### 12. Metrics and KPIs
- Top-level KPIs
- Channel-specific metrics
- Lead quality metrics
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Customer lifetime value (CLV)
- Marketing ROI
- Attribution model

### 13. Marketing Technology Stack
- CRM system
- Marketing automation
- Analytics tools
- Content management
- Social media management
- Email marketing platform
- SEO tools
- Advertising platforms

### 14. Timeline and Roadmap
- 90-day action plan
- Quarterly milestones
- Annual roadmap
- Campaign schedule
- Resource allocation timeline

### 15. Risk Analysis
- Potential challenges
- Mitigation strategies
- Contingency plans
- Market risks
- Competitive threats

### 16. Testing and Optimization
- A/B testing plan
- Optimization strategy
- Experimentation roadmap
- Learning agenda

### 17. Governance and Reporting
- Reporting frequency
- Stakeholder communication
- Review meetings
- Decision-making process
- Performance reviews

## Output Format

Provide a professional, actionable marketing plan with:
- Clear structure and headings
- Specific tactics and timelines
- Budget breakdowns
- Measurable objectives
- Visual descriptions (charts, tables, timelines)
- Prioritized recommendations

Include a one-page executive summary at the beginning and a 30-60-90 day quick-start action plan at the end.

Ask clarifying questions about the business, product, market, budget, and goals before generating the plan.
