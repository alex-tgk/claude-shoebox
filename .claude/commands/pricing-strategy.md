# Pricing Strategy Designer

Design a comprehensive pricing strategy and tier structure for the product or service specified by the user.

## Instructions

Develop a complete pricing strategy that includes:

### 1. Pricing Objectives
- Revenue goals
- Market penetration vs. profit maximization
- Competitive positioning goals
- Customer acquisition vs. lifetime value targets

### 2. Cost Analysis
- Fixed costs
- Variable costs per user/transaction
- Cost to serve by segment
- Unit economics and margins
- Break-even analysis

### 3. Value Analysis
- Customer perceived value
- Willingness to pay by segment
- Value metric identification (users, usage, features, outcomes)
- Value-based pricing opportunities

### 4. Competitive Pricing Landscape
- Competitor pricing ranges
- Market price expectations
- Price positioning strategy (premium, mid-market, value)
- Competitive differentiation justification

### 5. Pricing Model Selection
Evaluate and recommend:
- Subscription vs. one-time
- Usage-based pricing
- Freemium model
- Tiered pricing
- Per-user vs. per-feature
- Hybrid models

### 6. Pricing Tier Structure
Design 3-4 pricing tiers with:
- Tier names and positioning
- Price points for each tier
- Features included in each tier
- Target customer segment for each tier
- Upgrade incentives
- Feature gating strategy

### 7. Pricing Page Design
Recommendations for:
- Visual hierarchy
- Feature comparison table
- Call-to-action placement
- Social proof elements
- Frequently asked questions
- Annual vs. monthly toggle

### 8. Discount Strategy
- Volume discounts
- Annual subscription discounts
- Promotional pricing
- Educational/nonprofit pricing
- Early adopter pricing
- Enterprise custom pricing

### 9. Pricing Psychology
- Charm pricing tactics ($99 vs $100)
- Anchoring strategies
- Decoy pricing
- Price framing
- Value communication

### 10. Expansion Revenue Strategy
- Upsell opportunities
- Cross-sell opportunities
- Add-on products/features
- Professional services
- Premium support tiers

### 11. Price Testing Plan
- A/B testing recommendations
- Metrics to monitor
- Iteration strategy
- Grandfathering policy

### 12. Implementation Roadmap
- Pricing rollout plan
- Communication strategy
- Migration plan for existing customers
- Sales enablement
- Success metrics

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. Executive summary with recommended pricing strategy
2. Detailed pricing tier table
3. Pricing page wireframe description
4. Financial projections (revenue scenarios)
5. Competitive positioning analysis
6. Implementation timeline
7. Risk analysis and mitigation

Ask clarifying questions about the product, target market, costs, and business goals before designing the pricing strategy.
