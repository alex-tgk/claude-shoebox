# Media Press Kit Creator

Create a complete media/press kit with company overview, founder bios, product information, brand assets, press releases, media coverage, and downloadable resources for journalists and media professionals.

## Instructions

Generate a comprehensive press kit including:

### 1. Press Kit Strategy

#### Purpose:
- Make it easy for media to cover your company
- Provide ready-to-use assets and information
- Build brand consistency across coverage
- Establish credibility and authority
- Generate positive PR and visibility

#### Target Audience:
- Journalists and reporters
- Bloggers and content creators
- Podcast hosts
- Industry analysts
- Event organizers
- Potential partners

### 2. Company Overview & Boilerplate

#### Company Boilerplate (100 words):

```
About [Company Name]

[Company Name] is a [industry/category] company that [what you do in one sentence].

Founded in [year] by [founder names], [Company] helps [target audience] [achieve primary outcome] through [unique approach/technology].

With [number] customers in [number] countries, [Company] has [key achievement or metric]. The company has [raised $X in funding / is profitable / is bootstrapped] and is headquartered in [location].

For more information, visit [website] or follow us on [social platforms].
```

**Example:**
```
About Acme Software

Acme Software is a project management platform that helps remote teams stay organized and productive.

Founded in 2020 by Jane Smith and John Doe, Acme helps distributed teams collaborate seamlessly through AI-powered workflows and real-time communication tools.

With 50,000+ customers in 120 countries, Acme has facilitated over 10 million projects. The company raised $20M in Series A funding and is headquartered in San Francisco, CA.

For more information, visit acmesoftware.com or follow @acmesoftware on Twitter.
```

#### Extended Company Story (500 words):

**Structure:**

**The Origin Story:**
```
[Company Name] was born out of [founder's personal experience/problem].

[Compelling narrative about why the company was started]

[The "aha moment" that led to the solution]

[Early days and challenges overcome]
```

**The Mission:**
```
Our mission is to [mission statement].

We believe that [core belief about the industry/problem].

We're building [product/service] to [ultimate vision for change].
```

**The Product:**
```
[Product Name] is [description].

Unlike traditional solutions that [old way], [Product] [new way] by [unique approach].

Key features include:
• [Feature 1 and benefit]
• [Feature 2 and benefit]
• [Feature 3 and benefit]
```

**The Traction:**
```
Since launching in [year], we've:

• Grown [X]% year-over-year

```

```

### 3. Founder Bios and Photos

#### Founder Bio Template:

**Short Version (50 words):**
```
[Full Name], [Title]

[Name] is the [Title] of [Company]. Previously, [he/she/they] [previous notable experience]. [Name] holds a [degree] from [university] and has been recognized as [achievement/award]. [He/She/They] is based in [location].
```

**Extended Version (200 words):**
```
[Full Name], [Title] & [Role]

[Name] is the [Title] and [co-founder/founder] of [Company], where [he/she/they] [primary responsibility].

Before starting [Company], [Name] [previous career highlights]:
• [Achievement 1]
• [Achievement 2]
• [Achievement 3]

[Personal story that led to founding the company]

[Name]'s expertise in [domain] comes from [years] of experience in [industry], including roles at [previous companies]. [Notable projects or accomplishments].

[He/She/They] has been featured in [media outlets] and recognized as [awards or honors]. [Name] is a frequent speaker at [industry events] and contributor to [publications].

[Name] holds a [degree] in [field] from [university] and currently resides in [location].

Connect with [Name]:
• Email: [email]
• LinkedIn: [URL]
• Twitter: @[handle]

[Optional: Personal touch - interests, causes, fun fact]
```

**Example:**
```
Jane Smith, CEO & Co-Founder

Jane Smith is the CEO and co-founder of Acme Software, where she leads product vision and company strategy.

Before starting Acme, Jane spent 10 years in product management at Google and Salesforce, where she:
• Led the development of Google Workspace collaboration features used by 3B+ users
• Managed a $50M product line at Salesforce
• Filed 3 patents in distributed systems

The idea for Acme came from Jane's frustration managing remote teams across time zones. She realized existing tools were built for co-located teams and didn't address the unique challenges of distributed work.

Jane's expertise in collaboration software comes from 15 years of experience in enterprise SaaS, including building teams at Microsoft, Google, and Salesforce. She led the product that won "Best Collaboration Tool" at TechCrunch Disrupt 2018.

She has been featured in Forbes, TechCrunch, and The Wall Street Journal, and recognized as one of "40 Under 40" by Business Insider. Jane is a frequent speaker at SaaStr, Web Summit, and SXSW.

Jane holds a BS in Computer Science from Stanford University and an MBA from Harvard Business School. She currently resides in San Francisco.

Connect with Jane:
• Email: jane@acmesoftware.com
• LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/janesmith
• Twitter: @janesmith

When she's not building software, Jane mentors female founders and is an angel investor in 20+ startups.

[Compelling, News-Worthy Headline]
[Subheadline Providing Context]

[CITY, STATE] – [Date] – [Company Name], [one-line description], today announced [news].

[Opening paragraph: Who, what, when, where, why - most important info first]

[Second paragraph: Key details, context, significance]

"[Compelling quote from CEO/founder about the news and its impact]," said [Name], [Title] at [Company].

[Third paragraph: More details, data, or context]

[Fourth paragraph: What this means for customers/industry]

"[Quote from customer, partner, or industry expert]," said [Name], [Title] at [Company/Organization].

[Fifth paragraph: Additional context, future plans, or call-to-action]

For more information, visit [URL] or contact [contact information].

[Standard boilerplate - see section 2]

[Name]
