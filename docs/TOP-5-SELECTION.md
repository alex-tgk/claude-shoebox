# Top 5 Business Selection Analysis

## Evaluation Criteria

I analyzed all 60 PRDs across these dimensions:

### 1. **Revenue Potential** (0-10)
- Year 1 MRR/ARR potential
- Pricing power and customer lifetime value
- Market size and addressable audience

### 2. **Time to Market** (0-10)
- Speed to MVP (faster = higher score)
- Technical complexity
- Dependencies on external platforms

### 3. **Startup Cost** (0-10)
- Initial investment required (lower = higher score)
- Ongoing operational costs
- Cost to acquire first customer

### 4. **Scalability** (0-10)
- Ability to grow without linear effort
- Marginal cost per customer
- Automation potential

### 5. **Market Demand** (0-10)
- Validated demand and willingness to pay
- Growing vs shrinking market
- Pain point severity

### 6. **Competitive Advantage** (0-10)
- Differentiation from competitors
- Barriers to entry for others
- Network effects or moats

### 7. **Profit Margins** (0-10)
- Gross margin percentage
- Operating leverage
- Cost structure efficiency

### 8. **Solo Feasibility** (0-10)
- Can one person build and run it?
- Support burden
- Operational complexity
