# AI-Generated Online Course Platform

**Tagline:** Launch comprehensive online courses in days with AI-powered content creation—teach anything without being the expert

---

## 1. Business Overview

The online education market exceeds $300 billion globally, with platforms like Udemy, Teachable, and Coursera facilitating millions of course sales annually. However, creating quality courses is resource-intensive: filming videos takes weeks, writing scripts requires expertise, creating slides demands design skills, and course structuring requires instructional design knowledge.

This business uses AI to create complete, professional online courses on evergreen topics, then sells them on established marketplaces (Udemy, Skillshare) or through a personal platform. AI handles course outlining, script writing, slide generation, quiz creation, and even video narration. The solo entrepreneur focuses on niche research, AI prompt engineering, light editing, and marketing.

**Key Differentiator:** You're selling educational outcomes and structured knowledge, not "AI courses." Students care about learning skills and achieving results—whether the instructor used AI to create materials is irrelevant if the content delivers value.

## 2. Target Market

- Career switchers learning new job skills (data analysis, digital marketing, project management)

- Hobbyists pursuing personal development (photography, productivity, public speaking)

- Corporate licenses: $500-5,000 per course for team access

## 5. Revenue Model

**Udemy Model (Easiest Start):**
- **Revenue Share:** 50% of sales from your promotion, 25% from Udemy marketplace
- **Pricing:** Udemy sets sale prices ($9.99-19.99 typical)
- **Volume:** Need 100-500 sales per course for $1,000-5,000 revenue
- **Example:** 200 sales at $14.99 = $2,998 revenue × 37.5% avg. share = $1,124 per course

**Self-Hosted Model (Higher Margins):**
- **Course Pricing:** $47-297 per course (you keep 95% after payment fees)
- **Bundle Pricing:** 3-course bundle for $197-497
- **Subscription:** $29-79/month for access to all courses
- **Example:** 50 sales at $97 = $4,850 revenue × 95% = $4,607 profit

**Scaling Strategy:**
- Month 1-2: Launch first course on Udemy to validate demand
- Month 3-4: Create 2-3 more courses, build catalog
- Month 5-6: Launch own platform with bundles for higher margins
- Month 6-12: Create 10-15 courses generating combined $3,000-10,000/month

**Additional Revenue Streams:**
- **Coaching/Consulting:** Upsell 1-on-1 sessions ($100-500/hour)
- **Corporate Licensing:** Sell bulk access to companies ($1,000-10,000)
- **Course Updates:** Charge for major updates/certifications
- **Affiliate Commissions:** Recommend tools used in courses (10-30% commission)
- **Community Access:** Premium Slack/Discord for $20-50/month

**Minimal Investment Strategy:**
- Start with Udemy (free to publish, they handle everything)
- Use free AI tools (ChatGPT free tier, Canva free) initially
- Record yourself on camera (smartphone) to avoid AI voice costs
- Create 3-5 courses before investing in paid platform
- Reinvest first $1,000-2,000 in better equipment/subscriptions

## 6. Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: First Course Creation (Weeks 1-3)
- Week 1: Research profitable niches on Udemy, choose topic with proven demand
- Week 1: Use AI to generate comprehensive curriculum (30-50 lessons)
- Week 2: AI writes all lesson scripts, create presentation slides
- Week 2-3: Record videos (camera or screen recording), add voiceovers
- Week 3: Create quizzes, resources, upload to Udemy
- **Deliverable:** First complete course published on Udemy

### Phase 2: Scale Production (Weeks 4-8)
- Week 4-5: Create second course in related niche (cross-promotion)
- Week 6-7: Create third course, experiment with different format (screenshare vs. talking head)
- Week 8: Set up basic landing page for email collection
- **Deliverable:** 3 courses live, email list started

### Phase 3: Optimization & Platform (Weeks 9-12)
- Week 9: Analyze performance data, create improved version of best seller
- Week 10: Set up Teachable or custom platform for direct sales
- Week 11: Create course bundles, launch with email list
- Week 12: Implement Udemy ads and Facebook ads for traffic
- **Deliverable:** Dual revenue streams (Udemy + own platform)

### Phase 4: Advanced Monetization (Months 4-6)
- Month 4: Launch subscription model with monthly content drops
- Month 5: Reach out to companies for bulk licensing
- Month 6: Create certification program or advanced tier courses
- **Deliverable:** Diversified revenue beyond course sales
