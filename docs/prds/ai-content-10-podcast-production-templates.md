# AI-Generated Podcast Production Templates

**Tagline:** Complete podcast production kits—intro music, show notes, episode templates, and social media assets, all AI-generated

---

## 1. Business Overview

The podcast industry has exploded to over 5 million shows globally, with new podcasters launching daily. However, production quality separates successful podcasts from abandoned ones. Podcasters need professional intro/outro music, consistent show notes, social media graphics, episode planning templates, and branding—resources that typically cost $1,000-5,000 to create professionally.

Most podcasters either settle for generic production elements (making their show sound like everyone else's) or spend hours DIY-ing materials they're not skilled to create. This creates a significant opportunity for comprehensive, niche-specific podcast production kits.

This business uses AI to create complete podcast production packages: custom intro/outro music (via Suno/Udio), show notes templates (ChatGPT), episode planning worksheets, audiogram templates, social media graphics (Canva/Midjourney), and podcast artwork. Packages are sold as one-time purchases on Etsy/Gumroad or via subscription for ongoing content packs.

**Key Differentiator:** Complete production kits for specific podcast niches (true crime, business, wellness, comedy) rather than generic assets, plus AI-generated music that's unique to each customer.
