# AI-Generated Presentation Template Marketplace

**Tagline:** Professional PowerPoint and Keynote templates for every industry—designed by AI, customized by you

---

## 1. Business Overview

The presentation template market is a multi-million dollar industry, with professionals, entrepreneurs, students, and businesses constantly needing polished presentations for pitches, reports, training, and conferences. Creating professional presentations requires design expertise, understanding of visual hierarchy, and hours of formatting work—skills most people lack.

Traditional solutions include hiring designers ($500-2,000 per deck), using generic templates that everyone recognizes, or spending hours DIY-ing presentations. This creates an opportunity for unique, industry-specific templates that look custom-designed but are ready to use in minutes.

This business uses AI to design professional presentation templates for specific industries (tech startups, real estate, consulting, education, healthcare) and use cases (pitch decks, sales presentations, training materials, conference talks). AI handles layout design, color schemes, typography, and content structure, while templates are sold on Etsy, Creative Market, Envato Elements, or directly via Shopify/Gumroad.

**Key Differentiator:** Industry-specific templates with relevant slide types (SaaS: metrics slides, product roadmaps; Real Estate: property showcases, market analysis) rather than generic business templates.
