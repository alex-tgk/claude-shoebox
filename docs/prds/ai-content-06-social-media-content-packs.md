# AI-Generated Social Media Content Packs

**Tagline:** Pre-made Instagram posts, TikTok captions, and content calendars for niche businesses—created by AI, sold as ready-to-use packs

---

## 1. Business Overview

Social media management is a $17+ billion industry, with small businesses and solopreneurs struggling to maintain consistent posting. Creating engaging content requires copywriting skills, design abilities, hashtag research, and time—resources most busy entrepreneurs lack. Many hire social media managers ($500-3,000/month) or struggle with inconsistent posting that hurts their growth.

This business uses AI to create comprehensive social media content packs for specific niches: 30 days of Instagram posts with captions, relevant hashtags, TikTok video scripts, story templates, and posting schedules. AI tools handle copywriting (ChatGPT/Claude), graphic creation (Midjourney/Canva), hashtag research, and strategic planning. Packs are sold as digital downloads on Etsy, Gumroad, or directly to niche communities.

**Key Insight:** You're not selling AI tools—you're selling time-saving content solutions. A chiropractor or yoga studio doesn't care that AI created their captions; they care about having 30 days of professional content ready to post.

## 3. Core Features (MVP)

**Content Pack Components:**
- **30 Instagram Post Templates:** Designed graphics with editable text (Canva templates)
- **30 Carousel Post Ideas:** Educational or engagement-focused multi-image posts
- **30 Caption Sets:** Primary caption + 3-5 variations for each post
- **900 Hashtags:** 30 optimized hashtag sets (30 hashtags per post)
- **30 Instagram Story Templates:** Quick-post story designs with CTA
- **15 Reel/TikTok Script Ideas:** Short-form video scripts with hooks and CTAs
- **Content Calendar PDF:** Organized posting schedule with themes
- **Engagement Prompts:** Questions and conversation starters for stories
- **Bio Templates:** 5-10 optimized bio variations for profile

**Delivery System:**
- **Digital Download Platform:** Gumroad, Etsy, or Shopify for automated delivery
- **File Formats:** Canva link (editable templates), PDF (hashtags/captions), Google Doc (copy/paste)
- **Instant Access:** Automated delivery via email upon purchase
- **Update System:** Provide updated packs monthly for subscribers
- **Support Portal:** FAQs and tutorial videos for using templates

**Niche-Specific Customization:**
- **Industry-Relevant Content:** Healthcare tips for chiropractors, property trends for realtors
- **Seasonal Variations:** Holiday, summer, back-to-school themed content
- **Tone Matching:** Professional for lawyers, friendly for salons, motivational for coaches
- **Platform Optimization:** Different versions for Instagram vs. TikTok vs. LinkedIn
