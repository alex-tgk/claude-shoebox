# AI-Generated Infographic Template Service

**Tagline:** Professional data visualization and infographic templates for marketers—designed by AI, sold as ready-to-customize templates

---

## 1. Business Overview

Infographics are essential marketing tools, with visual content generating 94% more views than text alone. Businesses, marketers, educators, and content creators constantly need infographics for blog posts, social media, presentations, and reports. However, creating professional infographics requires design skills, data visualization knowledge, and hours of work—or hiring designers at $100-500 per infographic.

This business uses AI to design professional infographic templates that buyers can customize with their own data and branding. AI tools like ChatGPT plan layouts and content structure, Midjourney/DALL-E generates visual elements, and Canva Pro creates editable templates. Templates are sold on Etsy, Creative Market, Envato Elements, or directly via Gumroad for businesses and marketers to customize instantly.

**Key Insight:** You're not selling "AI-generated templates"—you're selling time-saving design solutions. A social media manager doesn't care that AI created the template; they care about having professional-looking infographics ready in minutes instead of hours.
