## 📋 What's Included in Each PRD

Every PRD contains:

1. **Project Title & Tagline** - Clear positioning
2. **Business Overview** - Market opportunity and problem statement
3. **Target Market** - Detailed customer personas with willingness to pay
4. **Core Features** - 5-7 MVP features with detailed specs
5. **Technical Stack** - Complete technology breakdown
6. **Revenue Model** - Pricing tiers, unit economics, projections
7. **Implementation Roadmap** - 3 phases from MVP to scale
8. **AI Integration Points** - 6+ specific AI use cases
9. **Time to MVP** - Realistic estimates for solo development
10. **Startup Costs** - Itemized budget under $500

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Browse the PRDs** - Read through ideas that interest you
2. **Validate the market** - Talk to potential customers
3. **Pick your stack** - Choose technologies you're comfortable with
4. **Build MVP** - Focus on core value proposition (2-3 months)
5. **Get first customers** - Manual outreach, content marketing
6. **Iterate based on feedback** - Let customers guide your roadmap
7. **Scale gradually** - Automate, market, expand features

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Start with one** - Don't try to build multiple products simultaneously
- **Talk to users early** - Validate assumptions before building
- **Keep it simple** - MVP should be minimal but valuable
- **Charge from day one** - Validates real demand
- **Use no-code where possible** - Landing pages, payments, analytics
- **Automate marketing** - SEO, content, social media from the start
- **Build in public** - Share your journey, attract early adopters
