- **Recurring Revenue:** Subscription or marketplace-based models

- **Scalable Products:** Digital goods with minimal marginal costs

## Critical Success Factors

### To Maximize Success:

**1. Start Small, Focus Deep**
- Pick ONE business model initially
- Master it before expanding
- Don't build portfolio Day 1

**2. Leverage AI Correctly**
- Use AI for boilerplate, not strategy
- Review and refine all AI-generated code
- AI accelerates, doesn't replace thinking

**3. Validate Before Scaling**
- Get 10 paying customers first
- Achieve product-market fit
- Then scale production

**4. Quality Over Quantity**
- 5 excellent templates > 20 mediocre ones
- Focus on usefulness, not just volume
- Polish matters

**5. Marketing is 50% of Success**
- Build distribution from day one
- Content marketing compounds
- Community building pays off

**6. Customer Success = Retention**
- Great documentation reduces support
- Responsive support builds loyalty
- Happy customers refer others

**7. Continuous Improvement**
- Gather feedback religiously
- Iterate based on usage data
- Add features customers actually want
