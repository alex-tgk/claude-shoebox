# AI-Powered eBook Publishing Business

**Tagline:** Publish profitable non-fiction eBooks on Amazon KDP—written by AI, earning passive royalty income forever

---

## 1. Business Overview

The digital publishing market generates over $20 billion annually, with Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) enabling anyone to publish and earn royalties without upfront costs. Non-fiction eBooks are particularly profitable: readers actively search for solutions to specific problems and are willing to pay $2.99-9.99 for expertise in digestible formats.

Traditional eBook creation requires months of writing, editing, formatting, and design—or hiring ghostwriters at $2,000-10,000 per book. AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude can now write comprehensive, well-researched non-fiction books in days for under $100. This business leverages AI to create high-quality eBooks on evergreen topics (productivity, personal finance, health, relationships), publishes them on Amazon KDP, and earns 70% royalty income passively.

**Key Differentiator:** You're not selling "AI-written books"—you're selling knowledge and solutions. Readers care about solving their problems (better sleep, starting a business, learning Excel), not whether a human or AI authored the content.

## 5. Revenue Model

**Amazon KDP Royalty Structure:**
- **70% Royalty:** $2.99-9.99 price range (recommended for maximum earnings)
  - Example: $4.99 book = $3.49 per sale
- **35% Royalty:** Under $2.99 or over $9.99
  - Example: $14.99 book = $5.25 per sale

**Kindle Unlimited (KU) Bonus Income:**
- Earn $0.004-0.005 per page read
- Example: 200-page book fully read = $0.80-1.00
- KU can double or triple total earnings vs. sales alone

**Revenue Projections Per Book:**
- **Conservative:** 30 sales/month + 500 page reads = $150-200/month per book
- **Moderate:** 100 sales/month + 2,000 page reads = $450-600/month per book
- **Success:** 300 sales/month + 5,000 page reads = $1,500-2,000/month per book

**Scaling Strategy:**
- **Month 1-2:** Publish first 3 books in related niche (build authority)
- **Month 3-4:** Publish 3 more books, create box set of first books
- **Month 5-6:** Reach 10 books, focus on marketing bestsellers
- **Month 6-12:** Publish 20-30 books total, generating $3,000-10,000/month combined

**Additional Revenue Streams:**
- **Paperback Sales:** KDP Print earns $3-8 per sale (lower royalty, higher price)
- **Audiobook Rights:** ACX/Findaway Voices for audio versions
- **Direct Sales:** Sell EPUB directly from website for 95% margins
- **Email List Monetization:** Promote affiliate products to readers
- **Course Creation:** Turn bestselling book into $97-297 course
- **Consulting/Coaching:** Upsell services related to book topics

**Minimal Investment Strategy:**
- Publish eBooks only initially (no print/audio costs)
- Use KDP Select for Kindle Unlimited exclusivity (bonus income)
- Use free ISBN from KDP (save $125+ per book)
- Create 5-10 books before heavy marketing spend
- Reinvest royalties into marketing and new book creation
